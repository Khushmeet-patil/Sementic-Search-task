This insurance policy applies to all registered gym members and covers injuries or medical expenses directly related to supervised physical activities within gym premises. Claims may be considered if the injury occurred during official workout sessions, group classes, or equipment usage under standard gym safety guidelines.

Members are required to report any injury to the management team within 24 hours. Delayed reporting may reduce eligibility for reimbursement or coverage review. Proof of medical diagnosis must be submitted to validate the claim.

The policy does not cover accidental injuries occurring outside gym property or during unsupervised activities. Pre-existing conditions such as diabetes, asthma, or chronic headaches are not eligible for reimbursement unless explicitly approved.

In case of medical intervention, recommended medicines such as ibuprofen must be purchased independently unless stated otherwise in a specific membership plan. Reimbursement decisions remain at the discretion of the gym’s insurance evaluator, based on injury severity and compliance with reporting procedures.

Gym members are encouraged to follow safety instructions, maintain proper hydration, and seek professional medical advice if symptoms persist. Regular health assessments can help prevent workout-related complications and ensure a safe gym environment.
