Membership refunds are eligible only within the first 15 days of enrollment. Refund requests must be submitted in writing along with proof of payment. Processing time may vary depending on staff workload and verification requirements.

If a member develops a medical condition that prevents continued exercise, partial refunds or membership extensions may be considered. A signed medical certificate confirming the condition, such as uncontrolled diabetes, severe asthma, or recurring headaches, must be submitted for review.

No refunds are issued for injuries caused by negligence, unsafe workouts, or misuse of gym equipment. Members are expected to follow all safety guidelines, warm-up routines, and hydration practices.

Refunds related to special promotional rates, discounted memberships, or group plans may be limited according to individual offer terms. The gym reserves the right to reject refund applications if necessary documentation is incomplete or submitted after the deadline.

For medicine purchases such as ibuprofen or personal health expenses, the gym does not provide refunds unless a written exception exists under a specific membership agreement. All decisions made by the refund panel are final and binding.
