Q: What should I do if I feel dizzy during exercise?
A: Stop activity immediately, hydrate, and rest. If symptoms persist, consult a medical professional. Dizziness can be related to blood sugar fluctuation, dehydration, or asthma-related breathing stress.

Q: Can I exercise if I have diabetes?
A: Yes, moderate exercise can help manage blood sugar levels. Diabetic members should monitor glucose readings before intense workouts and carry a quick carbohydrate source to prevent sudden drops.

Q: What medicine is good for workout-induced headaches?
A: Ibuprofen is commonly used for mild to moderate headache relief. However, frequent headaches during exercise may indicate dehydration, tension, or improper posture. Seek medical advice if headaches continue.

Q: Is asthma a concern while lifting weights?
A: Asthma is manageable with proper breathing techniques and rescue inhalers. Members should avoid dusty environments and take extra precautions during high-intensity workouts.

Q: Should I report injuries to the gym?
A: Yes, any injury occurring inside the gym should be reported to staff within 24 hours to qualify for insurance review. Accurate documentation ensures faster evaluation and treatment suggestions.
