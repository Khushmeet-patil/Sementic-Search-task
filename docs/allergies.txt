Allergies occur when the immune system reacts excessively to harmless substances such as pollen, food, dust mites, or animal dander. Symptoms vary from mild sneezing and itchy eyes to skin rashes and breathing difficulties. Allergies can affect people seasonally or throughout the year.

Understanding personal triggers allows individuals to avoid exposure and reduce discomfort. Over-the-counter antihistamines are commonly used to treat symptoms such as itching and congestion. Severe allergic reactions might require emergency care and injectable medication.

Environmental changes like using air filters, frequent cleaning, or wearing masks outdoors can reduce allergen exposure. In some cases, allergy testing helps identify specific triggers. Consulting a healthcare provider ensures appropriate long-term treatment.

Headaches can also accompany allergic reactions due to nasal congestion and sinus pressure. Individuals who frequently experience these symptoms should track environmental changes to identify recurring causes. Preventive strategies may improve overall comfort during allergy season.
