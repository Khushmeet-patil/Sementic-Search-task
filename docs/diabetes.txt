Diabetes is a long-term condition where the body struggles to regulate blood sugar effectively. People with diabetes either do not produce enough insulin or the insulin produced does not work efficiently. Keeping blood sugar levels stable is essential to prevent complications.

Common symptoms include frequent urination, increased thirst, fatigue, and slow wound healing. Some patients may also experience blurred vision or recurring infections. Early detection helps in managing the condition before it becomes severe.

A balanced diet with controlled carbohydrate intake is recommended for diabetic patients. Regular exercise can improve insulin sensitivity and help stabilize glucose levels. Monitoring blood glucose at home allows patients to understand how their lifestyle choices affect their readings.

In certain cases, medication or insulin therapy is prescribed by a doctor to maintain blood sugar levels. Skipping medication or unhealthy eating habits can cause sudden spikes or drops in glucose, which may require medical assistance. Diabetic individuals should consult a healthcare provider regularly for personalized care plans.
